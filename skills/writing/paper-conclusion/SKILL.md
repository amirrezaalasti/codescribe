# Paper Conclusion

## Purpose

Leave a clear takeaway: what was achieved, why it matters, and (optionally) what comes next — briefly.

## Structure

1. **Restate the problem and contribution** in 1–2 sentences
2. **Summarize main evidence** (no new numbers unless already shown)
3. **Broader takeaway**
4. **Future work** — 2–4 concrete directions max (or omit if discussed earlier)

## Instructions

1. Keep it short (often 1/2–1 page depending on venue).
2. Do not introduce new experiments or citations dump.
3. Mirror abstract/intro claims — consistency check.
4. Avoid “In this paper, we have proposed…” padding; prefer direct takeaways.
5. Future work should be actionable, not “more data, more experiments.”

## Mini shape

> We presented X to address Y. Across Z evaluations, X … These results suggest … Promising next steps include …

## Anti-patterns

- Full-paper recapitulation
- New claims not supported earlier
- Vague inspirational ending
- Contradicting limitations stated in discussion
