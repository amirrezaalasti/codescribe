# Discussion checklist

## Pass 1 — Interpretation

- [ ] Findings are interpreted (why this happened), not restated with synonyms of the results section
- [ ] Mechanisms are proposed for why baselines lose or win, when this can be reasoned about from the data
- [ ] Supported claims and speculation are visually or textually distinguished (e.g., "we hypothesize" vs. "the data show")

## Pass 2 — Relation to prior work

- [ ] Findings are related back to prior work: confirmed, extended, or contradicted
- [ ] Contradictions with prior work are addressed directly, not left implicit
- [ ] Alternative explanations for the results are considered when a reasonable one exists

## Pass 3 — Limitations

- [ ] Limitations are specific (data, scope, confounders, compute), not generic humility ("more work is needed")
- [ ] Populations/domains/settings not covered are named
- [ ] Assumptions that would break the method if violated are stated
- [ ] Possible evaluation artifacts that could explain the gains are considered and ruled in/out

## Pass 4 — Implications and scope

- [ ] Implications are grounded in the evidence actually shown, not extrapolated beyond it
- [ ] Tone stays measured — no marketing pivot ("this opens the door to...") unsupported by the results
- [ ] Future work (if included here rather than the conclusion) is prioritized, not a laundry list
- [ ] No new experimental claims appear that weren't in Results

See [examples.md](examples.md) for before/after rewrites and common failure patterns.
