# Paper Discussion

## Purpose

Explain what the results **mean**, where they **break**, and what they imply — without repeating the results section wholesale.

## Recommended structure

1. **Summary of key findings** (brief, claim-aligned)
2. **Interpretation** — mechanisms, why baselines lose/win
3. **Relation to prior work** — confirm, extend, or contradict
4. **Limitations / threats to validity** — honest and specific
5. **Implications** — practical or scientific so-what
6. **Optional future work** — only high-value next steps (or defer to conclusion)

## Instructions

1. Do not dump tables again; point back to results.
2. Separate **supported claims** from **speculation** (label clearly).
3. Limitations should be real (data, scope, confounders, compute), not fake humility.
4. Address alternative explanations when relevant.
5. Keep tone measured; avoid marketing pivots.

## Limitations prompts

- What populations/domains were not covered?
- What assumptions would break the method?
- What did you not measure?
- Could evaluation artifacts explain the gains?

## Anti-patterns

- Repeating results with synonyms
- Hiding critical limitations
- Over-promising impact
- Laundry-list future work with no prioritization
