# Related work checklist

## Pass 1 — Organization

- [ ] Organized by theme or dimension (e.g., scalability, supervision, guarantees), not by citation-list order
- [ ] Each theme groups papers that share an assumption or approach, not just a keyword
- [ ] Depth is proportional to relevance — tangential surveys get a pointer, not a subsection

## Pass 2 — Synthesis and fairness

- [ ] Each theme states what it achieves and what it leaves open before moving to the next
- [ ] The strongest baseline(s) are acknowledged, not just the ones easiest to beat
- [ ] No strawmanning — prior work's stated goals and constraints are represented accurately
- [ ] Comparisons use criteria that matter to this paper's claims (accuracy, cost, assumptions), not incidental ones

## Pass 3 — Positioning

- [ ] Every theme (or the section as a whole) ends with an explicit positioning statement: how this work relates
- [ ] The positioning names what's enabled by the difference, not just that a difference exists
- [ ] No claim of novelty that a cited paper already has

## Pass 4 — Citation hygiene

- [ ] No fabricated citations — uncertain references are marked `[CITATION NEEDED: description]`
- [ ] Paraphrase dominates; direct quotes are rare and justified
- [ ] Each citation is attached to the specific claim it supports, not a general paragraph

See [examples.md](examples.md) for before/after rewrites and common failure patterns.
