# Literature review checklist

## Pass 1 — Scope

- [ ] Review question, inclusion/exclusion criteria, time range, and venues searched are stated
- [ ] The search strategy is reproducible in principle (databases/venues, search terms, or a named methodology e.g. PRISMA/snowballing)
- [ ] Core sources are distinguished from peripheral ones, with peripheral ones summarized briefly or grouped

## Pass 2 — Synthesis

- [ ] Organized by theme, not one paragraph per paper
- [ ] Multiple works appear in the same sentence when they share a claim, method, or limitation
- [ ] Comparisons use shared criteria across papers (task, supervision, scale, guarantees) — not ad hoc per paper
- [ ] Conflicting findings across sources are named, with a candidate reason (different data, metric, population, definition)

## Pass 3 — Gap and bridge

- [ ] A gap statement names precisely what remains open and why it matters, not "more research is needed"
- [ ] If the review motivates present work, the bridge paragraph explains how the present work addresses the named gap
- [ ] The gap is not one already closed by a source the review itself cites

## Pass 4 — Citation hygiene

- [ ] No citation or finding invented from memory; uncertain ones are marked `[CITATION NEEDED]`
- [ ] Quotation is rare; paraphrase is accurate to the source's actual claim and scope
- [ ] A taxonomy figure/table is present if the review classifies more than ~10 sources

See [examples.md](examples.md) for before/after rewrites and common failure patterns.
